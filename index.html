Your readiness
What this is

Hazmat practice at the Awareness and Operations level: see it from a distance, look it up, set the zones, read the meter, protect people, and know when the right call is isolate, deny entry, call the hazmat team. Built around the workflow. On a real call, use your department's current ERG or the PHMSA ERG app.

Drill night

Live injects (a wind shift, a second container, a symptomatic bystander, a meter alarm) and a freeze while an incident runs. On by itself during a Drill Night. Arrives with the first incidents.

More

A practice tool. It does not replace an official course, a department's procedures, or the current Emergency Response Guidebook, and it does not issue certification. Operations-level responders act defensively, from outside the hazard, and call a hazmat team for anything more. Not affiliated with or endorsed by PHMSA, the NFPA, or any hazmat team.
